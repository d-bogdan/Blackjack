blackjack

Scoring:

  • Ace = 1 or 11
  • Face Cards = 10
  • Number Cards = Value of card

Add bets:

  • only at the beginning
  • no side bets
  • you can double -> in case the same card is received

Dealer:

  • Dealer shows one card at the start
  • Dealer must hit until reaching 17 or higher

Win condition:

  • hit blackjack(21)
  • dealer busts and you did not bust as well
  • sum bigger than the dealer without busting

Payout:

  • double the bet if you win
  • lose the bet if you lose - simple
  • no insurance
